Explain it simply (Feynman)

What do “strong”, “moderate” and “limited” evidence mean?

Strong: helped in many studies, with different learners and subjects. Moderate: helped in a good number of studies, or only for some kinds of material. Limited: little direct research or mixed results. That doesn’t mean useless: Pomodoro, for example, is a good way to start work and avoid distraction. The ratings follow Dunlosky et al. (2013), a review whose studies were mostly with older students. The time shown is for one study session.
