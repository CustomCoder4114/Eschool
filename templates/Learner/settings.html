Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information Eschool collects about you, why we need it, who can see it, how long we keep it and the choices you have. We have written it so that a learner can understand it. If anything is unclear, ask a parent, guardian or teacher to read it with you, or contact us through Help & Support.

In this policy

1. Who we are

Eschool is an online learning platform for learners, parents and guardians, and teachers. In this policy, "Eschool", "we" and "us" mean the team that runs the platform, and "you" means the person using the account. We decide how your personal information is used, so we are responsible for looking after it. We handle personal information in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and the other laws that apply to us.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your first and last name, email address, password and the type of account (learner, parent or teacher). Your password is never shown to anyone, including your parents and Eschool staff.
  • Personal information you choose to add: phone number, date of birth, gender, country, school, grade, a guardian's name and phone number, time zone and a short bio.
  • Badge picture: the badge you show as your picture (learners don't upload photos).
  • Things you create or send: messages, community study tips, resources you upload, schedule events, support tickets and your replies, likes, saves, friends and the teachers you follow.

Information created when you learn

  • Courses you enrol in, modules you finish, module test and quiz scores, the Daily Quiz Test, grades and GPA.
  • Eschool Points (EP), your level, monthly rank, streaks, achievements and badges, and items you get from the Points shop.
  • How long you study, the days you are active, and goals a linked parent sets for you and your progress on them.

Information about your use of Eschool

  • When you sign in and out, the devices and browsers you sign in from, and your active sessions (shown under Account & Security).
  • Notifications we send you and whether you have read them.
  • Basic technical records such as error logs, which we use to keep the platform working.

We do not collect your exact location, your contacts or anything from other apps on your device.

3. How we use your information

  • To run your account and lessons: signing you in, saving your progress, marking tests and quizzes, and showing your courses, schedule and resources.
  • To award EP and achievements: working out your points, level, streaks, badges and your place on the monthly leaderboard.
  • To connect people: messages, friends, following teachers, community tips and teacher resources.
  • To keep you informed: notifications about new lessons, reminders, replies from support and goals, as you choose under Notifications.
  • To keep Eschool safe: protecting accounts, stopping misuse and bullying, and looking into reports.
  • To help you: answering support tickets.
  • To improve Eschool: understanding which features and lessons work well, using combined information that does not single you out.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to show you advertising from other companies.

4. What other people on Eschool can see

You control most of this in Settings > Privacy, and every choice is checked on our servers:

  • Who can open your profile: everyone, only teachers and your parents, or only you and your linked parents.
  • What your profile shows: you can hide your level and EP, your achievements and badges, your monthly rank, your quiz streak and your friends list.
  • The leaderboard: you can hide your name and EP from the monthly leaderboard. You still earn EP and achievements.
  • Search: you can stop people finding you when they search.
  • Messages: you choose who can start a conversation with you.

Friends and teachers: learners become friends only when one sends a friend request and the other accepts it; either of you can remove the friendship at any time. Following a teacher needs no approval. Parents can't be added as friends or followed.

Your name and badge picture can appear next to things you post, such as community tips, messages you send and resources you upload. Teachers can see the learning progress of learners in their classes so they can help them.

5. Parents and guardians

A parent or guardian can only link to your account after you agree, using a one-time code sent to your notification bell or a request you accept. When the link is made, the parent chooses what they can see, and you can always check this in Settings > Privacy under Parent / Guardian Access. You are told when it changes.

  • A linked parent can always see your name, badge picture, plan, the teachers you follow, your activity counts (modules done and streaks) and your profile, even if it is private.
  • Depending on what they chose, a linked parent may also see your grades and course progress, schedule, resources, updates about your activity and who you talk to.
  • A parent can set study goals and rewards, a daily time limit, quiet hours or a pause.
  • A parent can never read your password, sign in as you, change your settings or privacy choices, or post, message, add friends, follow or spend EP as you.

6. Learners under 18

Many of our learners are children. We collect only what we need to teach and keep learners safe, we do not show advertising to learners, and the safest privacy choices are always available in Settings. Where the law requires a parent's or guardian's consent to use a child's information, we rely on that consent, and a parent or guardian can contact us at any time about their child's information.

7. Who we share it with

  • Service providers who help us run Eschool, such as hosting, email delivery and payment processing. They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • Your linked parents and your teachers, as described above.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or when we need to protect someone's safety, for example to report a child at risk.
  • A new owner, if Eschool is ever merged or sold. This policy would continue to protect your information.

8. Payments

If you or a parent pay for a plan, the payment is processed through M-Pesa. We keep the phone number used, the amount, the plan and the transaction reference so we can confirm the payment and help with any problem. We never see or store M-Pesa PINs.

9. Information kept on your device

Your browser keeps some settings on the device you use so Eschool remembers them: your theme, accent colour, text size, session timeout and recently viewed courses. These stay on that device and can be removed by clearing your browser data. We use a sign-in cookie to keep you signed in; we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

10. How long we keep it

  • We keep your account information and learning records for as long as your account is open, so your progress, EP and achievements are not lost.
  • Read notifications can be deleted by you at any time. Support tickets are kept so we can help you again if the same problem returns.
  • When an account is closed, we delete or anonymise the personal information in it, except what we must keep by law (for example payment records) or to settle a dispute.

11. How we protect it

We use secure connections, limit which staff can see personal information, check privacy choices on our servers rather than only in your browser, and let you sign out of every device from Account & Security. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

12. Your rights and choices

You have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your information is used, which is what this policy is for.
  • Get a copy of your information: use Download my data in Settings > Privacy.
  • Correct it: change your details in Settings > Profile, or ask us.
  • Object or limit how it is used, for example by changing your privacy and notification choices.
  • Ask us to delete it or close your account, by contacting Help & Support. If you are a learner, we may check with your linked parent first.
  • Complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in Kenya if you are unhappy with how we handled your information. We would like the chance to fix it first, so please contact us.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Eschool changes or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is important, we will tell you in your notification bell before it takes effect.

14. Contact us

For any question or request about your privacy, open Help & Support and send a ticket under "My account, privacy or parents". We usually answer within one working day.

Terms of Service

Last updated: 25 October 2026

These terms are the rules for using Eschool. By creating an account or using Eschool you agree to them and to our Privacy Policy. If you are under 18, please read them with a parent or guardian, who also agrees to them on your behalf.

In these terms

1. Using Eschool

Eschool gives you courses, module tests, quizzes, resources from teachers, study strategies, a schedule, messages and progress tracking. You may use it for your own learning. You must give true information when you sign up and use only one learner account.

2. Your account

  • Keep your password secret and don't share your account. You are responsible for what happens on it.
  • If you think someone else has used your account, change your password and use Log out from all devices in Account & Security, then tell us.
  • Keep your details up to date in Settings > Profile.

3. Learning, tests and grades

  • Module tests are passed with 60% or more and can be retaken; your best score counts. Each course quiz and the Daily Quiz Test can be taken once a day.
  • Do your own work. Getting someone else to take your tests, sharing answers, or using tricks to change scores is cheating, and those results, EP and achievements can be removed.
  • Grades, GPA and progress on Eschool show how you are doing on the platform. They are not official school results or certificates unless your school tells you otherwise.

4. Eschool Points, levels and the shop

  • Eschool Points (EP) are earned by learning, as explained on the Points page. They have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold, swapped for money or moved to another account.
  • EP can be spent in the Points shop on profile titles, avatar frames and days of Eschool Basic. Spending EP does not lower your total EP, level or monthly rank.
  • The leaderboard ranks EP earned in the current month and starts again on the 1st (Kenya time).
  • If EP, a streak or an achievement was given by mistake or through cheating, we may correct or remove it.

5. Plans and payments

  • Some features need a paid plan. The price and what is included are shown before you pay.
  • Payments are made through M-Pesa. A plan starts once the payment is confirmed and lasts for the period shown.
  • If you were charged but your plan did not start, or you were charged twice, contact Help & Support with the M-Pesa transaction message and we will sort it out.
  • Learners should ask a parent or guardian before paying for anything.

6. Community rules

Eschool is a place to learn. When you message people, share tips or upload anything, you agree to:

  • Be kind and respectful. No bullying, threats, hate speech or harassment.
  • Keep it appropriate for learners. No sexual, violent or frightening content.
  • Protect privacy. Don't share anyone's personal details, photos or messages without their permission, and never share your own home address or passwords.
  • Be honest. Don't pretend to be someone else, including a teacher, parent or Eschool staff.
  • No spam, advertising, scams or links to harmful sites.
  • Don't try to break, overload or get around Eschool's security or parental controls.

7. Content you share

You own what you create, such as your tips, messages and uploads. By sharing it on Eschool, you allow us to store it and show it to the people you shared it with, so the platform can work. Only share content you have the right to share. We may remove content that breaks these terms.

8. Our content

Courses, lessons, tests, quizzes, study strategies and the Eschool name and design belong to Eschool or the teachers who made them. Resources from teachers are for viewing on Eschool for your own learning. Don't copy, download, sell or share them outside Eschool without permission.

9. Parents and guardians

A parent or guardian who links to a learner's account agrees to these terms for that learner and is responsible for the learner's use of Eschool. Parents can see what the learner allowed when linking, set goals and rewards, and set time limits, quiet hours or pauses. Parents must not use their link to sign in as the learner or to act for them.

10. Reporting and safety

If someone makes you feel unsafe or breaks these rules, send an Urgent ticket in Help & Support under "Report a person or content", and tell a parent, teacher or adult you trust. You can also call Childline Kenya on 116, free, any time. We look at every report and may warn, restrict or remove accounts. Where someone may be in danger, we may contact the right authorities.

11. Suspending or closing accounts

  • We may limit, suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, puts others at risk, or is used for cheating or fraud. Where we can, we will tell you why.
  • If you want your account closed or your information deleted, contact Help & Support. For a learner, we may confirm the request with a linked parent first.
  • When an account is closed, its EP, achievements and shop items are lost and cannot be moved to another account.

12. Availability and changes to Eschool

We work to keep Eschool available and correct, but it may sometimes be unavailable for maintenance or because of problems outside our control, and lessons may have mistakes. We may add, change or remove features and courses. If a change affects a paid plan you already have, we will make sure you get what you paid for or a fair alternative.

13. Our responsibility to you

We provide Eschool with reasonable care and skill. As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for losses that were not foreseeable, that are caused by your breaking these terms, or that are caused by events outside our control. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under Kenyan consumer law that cannot be limited.

14. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms when Eschool or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is important, we will tell you in your notification bell before it takes effect. If you keep using Eschool after that, the new terms apply.

15. Law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya. If you have a problem, please contact us first so we can try to fix it. If we cannot agree, the courts of Kenya can decide.

16. Contact us

Questions about these terms? Open Help & Support and send a ticket. We usually answer within one working day.
