Policies

The rules for using Eschool, and how your family's data is handled.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. What we collect

Details you give us (your name, email address, phone number, and location) and information created as you use Eschool, such as sign-in times, messages, payments you make and the settings you choose for your children.

2. Parents and children's data

A parent can only link to a child after the child agrees. The child decides what to share (grades and progress, schedule, attendance, resources, activity updates and messages) and can change it at any time.
  • Controls you set on Manage learner (screen time, contact rules, features) are stored so Eschool can enforce them, and your child is told when they change.
  • Goals and rewards you set are visible to the child they are for.
  • We never show you your child's password, and you can't sign in as them.

3. How we use information

  • To run Eschool and show you your children's progress
  • To send the notifications you chose, plus safety and payment notices
  • To keep accounts secure (sign-in alerts, automatic sign-out)
  • To process subscription and teaching payments (M-Pesa codes are checked by our team)

4. Sharing

We don't sell personal information. Teachers you hire see your name and the child they teach. Other people see your profile only as allowed in Privacy & data.

5. Keeping and deleting data

We keep your information while your account is active. Deactivating hides your profile but keeps your data. Deleting your account removes your personal data, your links to your children and the controls and goals you set, except where the law requires us to keep records such as payments.

6. Your choices

You can change your details, notifications and privacy here at any time, and download a copy of your data from Privacy & data.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to our team through Help & Support.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Agreeing to these terms

By creating an account and using Eschool you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy.

2. Your account

Keep your password private. You're responsible for what happens on your account, including payments and the controls you set for your children. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used it.

3. Parent accounts

  • Only link to children you are the parent or legal guardian of, or are responsible for with the family's permission.
  • Use what you see about your child to support their learning. Don't share it publicly.
  • Teachers you hire through Eschool are paid for the sessions you buy. Report any concern about a session from the Teachers page.

4. Payments

Subscriptions and teaching sessions start once your M-Pesa payment is confirmed. Payments that can't be confirmed are not charged on Eschool's side. Ask Help & Support about refunds.

5. Acceptable use

  • Don't misuse Eschool, try to get around security or parental controls, or interfere with other people's use
  • Don't post anything unlawful, abusive or that breaks someone else's privacy
  • Course materials can't be shared outside Eschool without permission

6. Suspension and closing accounts

We may suspend accounts that break these terms. You can deactivate or delete your account at any time from Sign-in & security.

7. Service and liability

Eschool is provided as it is. We work to keep it running but can't promise it will never be interrupted. As far as the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect losses from using it.

8. Changes

We may update these terms. We'll show the new date here, and using Eschool after a change means you accept it.
