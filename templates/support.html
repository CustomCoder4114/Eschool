About Eschool FAQs

What is Eschool?
Eschool is a modern digital learning platform built to make high-quality education accessible to every learner, everywhere
Is Eschool Free?
Yes! Eschool offers free courses, resources, career development and skills learning. You can upgrade to unlock unlimited features and free Ad learning.
Does it work on mobile devices?
Yes, Eschool works seamlessly across all devices- phones, tablets and Laptop. Eschool ensures you can study anywhere, anytime.

Learning & Courses

How do I track my course progress?
Your progress is automatically saved as you complete modules and exercises. Check the "Progress" tab to see your overall progress and completed modules for each course.
Can I retake quizzes and exercises?
Yes! You can retake exercises only as many times as you want to improve your score and understanding. Quizes are taken daily and you should keep your quiz streak!
How do I get certificates?
Certificates are awarded upon completing all modules, exercises, and passing the final assessment. You can download your certificate from the course completion page.

Technical Issues

The uploaded resource is taking long to upload?
This is actualy normal and if you are uploading a file with a very big storage this is very normal. Just wait and don't close that window.
My progress isn't being saved. How can I fix this?
Ensure you're logged in and have cookies enabled. Try logging out and back in, or contact support if the issue continues. Your progress is automatically saved in the cloud.
