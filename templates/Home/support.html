Common questions

Quick answers

Can't find what you need? Send us a message and we'll get back to you, usually within one working day.

How do I create an account?

Press Register, fill in your name, email and a password, and choose whether you are a learner, a parent or a teacher. You can also sign in with Google.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Send us a message from Contact us using the email address you signed up with, and our team will help you get back into your account. If you signed up with Google, use "Continue with Google" on the sign-in page instead.

How do I change my details or close my account?

Sign in and open Settings. You can change your profile, notifications and privacy there. Parents can deactivate or delete their account under Sign-in & security. Learners and teachers can ask Help & Support to close their account; for a learner we may check with a linked parent first.

How does a parent link to their child?

From a parent account, open Link a child. Your child confirms with a one-time code sent to their notification bell, or by accepting your request. Nothing is linked without your child agreeing.

What can a parent see?

The child chooses what to share when linking: grades and progress, schedule, attendance, resources, activity updates and messages. They can change it at any time and are told when it changes. A parent can set goals and rewards, a daily time limit, quiet hours or a pause, but can never see the child's password or sign in as them.

How do I pay for a plan or lessons?

You pay through eSchool, and the ways you can pay are shown before you pay, along with the price and what is included. A plan or lessons start once the payment is confirmed. Learners should ask a parent or guardian before paying for anything.

I paid but nothing changed.

Contact us with your payment receipt or reference. If you were charged twice, or your plan or lessons did not start, we will sort it out. We never ask for your PIN, card details or password.

How do private lessons with a teacher work?

A parent sends a request from the Teachers page. If the teacher accepts, they set the price per lesson. The parent pays eSchool, not the teacher, and the lessons start once the payment is confirmed. eSchool pays the teacher after the lessons. The parent can see every lesson, message and call between the teacher and their child.

How do I teach on eSchool?

Register as a teacher, then fill in your teaching profile in Settings: subjects, grades, qualifications, fee and availability. You can upload resources, run live classes and accept private lesson requests from parents. Your qualifications and experience must be true.

What are eSchool Points?

Learners earn eSchool Points (EP) by learning: finishing modules, passing tests and keeping streaks. EP can be spent in the Points shop. They have no cash value and can't be bought, sold or moved to another account.

Are eSchool results official school results?

No. Scores and progress on eSchool show how a learner is doing on the platform. They are not official school results or certificates unless your school tells you otherwise.

How do I report someone or something?

Signed in, open Help & Support and send an Urgent ticket under "Report a person or content". Not signed in? Use Contact us. We look at every report.

Policies

Our policies, in plain language

These apply to everyone who uses eSchool. Parents and teachers have a few extra rules, in their own tabs.

    Privacy policy

    Last updated: 28 October 2026

    This policy explains what information eSchool collects, why we need it, who can see it, how long we keep it and the choices you have. We have written it so that a learner can understand it. If anything is unclear, ask a parent, guardian or teacher to read it with you, or contact us.

    1Who we are

    eSchool is an online learning platform for learners, parents and guardians, and teachers. In this policy, "eSchool", "we" and "us" mean the team that runs the platform, and "you" means the person using the account. We decide how your personal information is used, so we are responsible for looking after it. We handle personal information in line with the data protection laws that apply to us.

    2Information we collect

    Information you give us

    • Account details: your first and last name, email address, password and the type of account (learner, parent or teacher). Your password is never shown to anyone, including parents and eSchool staff.
    • Details you choose to add: phone number, date of birth, gender, country, school, grade, a guardian's name and phone number, time zone and a short bio. Teachers also add a teaching profile: headline, subjects, grades, qualifications, fee and availability.
    • Profile photo if you upload one.
    • Things you create or send: messages, community study tips, resources you upload, live classes, schedule events, support tickets and your replies, reviews, likes, saves, friends and the teachers you follow.

    Information created when you learn

    • Courses you enrol in, modules you finish, and your module test, quiz and Daily Quiz Test scores.
    • eSchool Points (EP), your level, monthly rank, streaks, achievements and badges, and items you get from the Points shop.
    • How long you study, the days you are active, and goals a linked parent sets for you and your progress on them.

    Information about your use of eSchool

    • When you sign in and out, the devices and browsers you sign in from, and your active sessions.
    • Notifications we send you and whether you have read them.
    • Basic technical records such as error logs, which we use to keep the platform working.

    We do not collect your exact location, your contacts or anything from other apps on your device.

    3How we use your information

    • To run your account and lessons: signing you in, saving your progress, marking tests and quizzes, and showing your courses, schedule and resources.
    • To award EP and achievements: working out points, levels, streaks, badges and the monthly leaderboard.
    • To connect people: messages, friends, following teachers, live classes, private lessons and teacher resources.
    • To keep you informed: the notifications you chose, plus safety, account and payment notices.
    • To keep eSchool safe: protecting accounts, stopping misuse and bullying, and looking into reports.
    • To help you: answering support tickets and messages.
    • To improve eSchool: understanding which features and lessons work well, using combined information that does not single you out.

    We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it to show you advertising from other companies.

    4What other people on eSchool can see

    You control most of this in Settings > Privacy, and every choice is checked on our servers:

    • Who can open your profile: everyone, only teachers and your parents, or only you and your linked parents.
    • What your profile shows: learners can hide their level and EP, achievements and badges, monthly rank, quiz streak and friends list.
    • The leaderboard: learners can hide their name and EP from the monthly leaderboard.
    • Search and messages: you can stop people finding you in search, and choose who can start a conversation with you.

    Friends and teachers: learners become friends only when one sends a friend request and the other accepts it; either can remove the friendship at any time. Following a teacher needs no approval. Parents can't be added as friends or followed.

    A teacher's public profile shows their name, photo, headline, subjects, grades, qualifications, public resources, live classes, rating and reviews. A teacher's email, phone number and address are never shown.

    5Parents and guardians

    A parent or guardian can only link to a learner's account after the learner agrees, using a one-time code sent to their notification bell or a request they accept. The learner chooses what is shared and can change it at any time.
    • A linked parent can always see the learner's name, profile photo, plan, the teachers they follow, their activity counts (modules done and streaks) and their profile, even if it is private.
    • Depending on what was shared, a linked parent may also see grades and course progress, schedule, attendance, resources, activity updates and who the learner talks to.
    • A parent can set study goals and rewards, a daily time limit, quiet hours or a pause. The learner is told when controls change.
    • A parent can never read the learner's password, sign in as them, change their settings or privacy choices, or post, message, add friends, follow or spend EP as them.

    6Learners under 18

    Many of our learners are children. We collect only what we need to teach and keep learners safe, we do not show advertising to learners, and the safest privacy choices are always available in Settings. Where the law requires a parent's or guardian's consent to use a child's information, we rely on that consent, and a parent or guardian can contact us at any time about their child's information.

    7Who we share it with

    • Service providers who help us run eSchool, such as hosting, email delivery and payment processing. They may only use information to provide their service to us.
    • Linked parents, teachers you learn with, and parents you teach, as described in this policy.
    • Our team may look at a resource, review or message that has been reported, to keep eSchool safe.
    • Authorities, when the law requires it, or when we need to protect someone's safety, for example to report a child at risk.
    • A new owner, if eSchool is ever merged or sold. This policy would continue to protect your information.

    8Payments

    Plans and private lessons are paid through eSchool, using the payment methods shown when you pay. We keep the amount, what it was for, the payment reference and, for mobile money, the phone number used, so we can confirm the payment and help with any problem. Card payments are handled by our payment provider: we never see or store full card numbers, PINs or passwords.

    9Information kept on your device

    Your browser keeps some settings on the device you use so eSchool remembers them: your theme, accent colour, text size, session timeout and recently viewed courses. These stay on that device and can be removed by clearing your browser data. We use a sign-in cookie to keep you signed in; we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

    10How long we keep it

    • We keep account information and learning records while the account is open, so progress, EP and achievements are not lost.
    • You can delete read notifications at any time. Support tickets are kept so we can help again if the same problem returns.
    • Deactivating an account hides the profile but keeps the data. When an account is deleted or closed, we delete or anonymise the personal information in it, except what we must keep by law (for example payment records) or to settle a dispute.

    11How we protect it

    We limit which staff can see personal information, check privacy choices on our servers rather than only in your browser, send sign-in alerts, sign inactive sessions out automatically, and let you sign out of every device from your security settings. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

    12Your rights and choices

    • Be informed about how your information is used, which is what this policy is for.
    • Get a copy of your information: use Download my data in Settings > Privacy.
    • Correct it: change your details in Settings, or ask us.
    • Object or limit how it is used, for example by changing your privacy and notification choices.
    • Ask us to delete it or close your account. If you are a learner, we may check with your linked parent first.
    • Complain to the data protection authority where you live if you are unhappy with how we handled your information. We would like the chance to fix it first, so please contact us.

    13Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy when eSchool changes or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is important, we will tell you in your notification bell before it takes effect.

    14Contact us

    For any question or request about your privacy, send a ticket from Help & Support when signed in, or use Contact us. We usually answer within one working day.

    Terms of service

    Last updated: 28 October 2026

    These terms are the rules for using eSchool. By creating an account or using eSchool you agree to them and to our Privacy policy. If you are under 18, please read them with a parent or guardian, who also agrees to them on your behalf. Parents and teachers also follow the extra rules in their own tabs.

    1Using eSchool

    eSchool gives you courses, module tests, quizzes, resources from teachers, live classes, study strategies, a schedule, messages and progress tracking. You must give true information when you sign up and use only one account of each type.

    2Your account

    • Keep your password secret and don't share your account. You are responsible for what happens on it.
    • If you think someone else has used your account, change your password, sign out of all devices, then tell us.
    • Keep your details up to date in Settings.

    3Learning, tests and scores

    • Module tests are passed with 60% or more and can be retaken; your best score counts. Each course quiz and the Daily Quiz Test can be taken once a day.
    • Do your own work. Getting someone else to take your tests, sharing answers, or using tricks to change scores is cheating, and those results, EP and achievements can be removed.
    • Scores and progress on eSchool show how you are doing on the platform. They are not official school results or certificates unless your school tells you otherwise.

    4eSchool Points, levels and the shop

    • eSchool Points (EP) are earned by learning. They have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold, swapped for money or moved to another account.
    • EP can be spent in the Points shop on profile titles, avatar frames and days of eSchool Basic. Spending EP does not lower your total EP, level or monthly rank.
    • The leaderboard ranks EP earned in the current month and starts again on the 1st of each month (East Africa Time).
    • If EP, a streak or an achievement was given by mistake or through cheating, we may correct or remove it.

    5Plans and payments

    • Some features need a paid plan. The price and what is included are shown before you pay.
    • Payments are made through eSchool, using the payment methods shown when you pay. A plan or private lessons start once the payment is confirmed and last for the period or number of lessons shown.
    • Payments that can't be confirmed are not charged on eSchool's side. If you were charged but nothing started, or you were charged twice, contact us with your payment receipt or reference and we will sort it out.
    • Learners should ask a parent or guardian before paying for anything.

    6Community rules

    eSchool is a place to learn. When you message people, share tips, teach or upload anything, you agree to:

    • Be kind and respectful. No bullying, threats, hate speech or harassment.
    • Keep it appropriate for learners. No sexual, violent or frightening content.
    • Protect privacy. Don't share anyone's personal details, photos or messages without their permission, and never share your own home address or passwords.
    • Be honest. Don't pretend to be someone else, including a teacher, parent or eSchool staff. Don't post, buy or ask for fake reviews.
    • No spam, advertising, scams or links to harmful sites.
    • Don't try to break, overload or get around eSchool's security, privacy choices or parental controls.

    7Content you share

    You own what you create, such as your tips, messages and uploads. By sharing it on eSchool, you allow us to store it and show it to the people you shared it with, so the platform can work. Only share content you have the right to share. We may remove content that breaks these terms.

    8Our content

    Courses, lessons, tests, quizzes, study strategies and the eSchool name and design belong to eSchool or the teachers who made them. Resources from teachers are for viewing on eSchool for your own learning. Don't copy, sell or share them outside eSchool without permission.

    9Reporting

    If someone breaks these rules, send an Urgent ticket in Help & Support under "Report a person or content", or use Contact us if you are not signed in. We look at every report and may warn, restrict or remove accounts. Where the law requires it, we may contact the right authorities.

    10Suspending or closing accounts

    • We may limit, suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, puts others at risk, or is used for cheating or fraud. Where we can, we will tell you why.
    • You can ask us to close your account or delete your information. For a learner, we may confirm the request with a linked parent first.
    • When a learner account is closed, its EP, achievements and shop items are lost and cannot be moved to another account.

    11Availability and changes to eSchool

    We work to keep eSchool available and correct, but it may sometimes be unavailable for maintenance or because of problems outside our control, and lessons may have mistakes. We may add, change or remove features and courses. If a change affects a paid plan you already have, we will make sure you get what you paid for or a fair alternative.

    12Our responsibility to you

    We provide eSchool with reasonable care and skill. As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for losses that were not foreseeable, that are caused by your breaking these terms, or that are caused by events outside our control. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under the consumer laws where you live that cannot be limited.

    13Changes to these terms

    We may update these terms when eSchool or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is important, we will tell you in your notification bell before it takes effect. If you keep using eSchool after that, the new terms apply.

    14Law and disputes

    These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya. If you have a problem, please contact us first so we can try to fix it. If we cannot agree, the courts of Kenya can decide.

    15Contact us

    Questions about these terms? Send a ticket from Help & Support when signed in, or use Contact us. We usually answer within one working day.

    Extra rules for parents and guardians

    Last updated: 28 October 2026

    These rules are added to the Privacy policy and Terms of service for anyone using a parent account.

    1Linking to a child

    • Only link to children you are the parent or legal guardian of, or are responsible for with the family's permission.
    • A link is only made after the child agrees. The child decides what to share and can change it at any time.
    • By linking, you agree to these terms for that child and are responsible for their use of eSchool.

    2What you see and how to use it

    • Use what you see about your child to support their learning. Don't share it publicly.
    • We never show you your child's password, and you can't sign in as them or act for them.

    3Controls, goals and rewards

    • Controls you set (screen time, quiet hours, pauses, contact rules and features) are stored so eSchool can enforce them, and your child is told when they change.
    • Goals and rewards you set are visible to the child they are for.

    4Private teachers

    • Teachers you hire see your name and the child they teach, and you see every lesson, message and call between the teacher and your child.
    • Pay through eSchool, not the teacher directly. Teachers are paid for the lessons you buy.
    • Report any concern about a lesson from the Teachers page. Lessons paid for but not taught can be refunded through Help & Support.

    5Your data and your account

    Deleting your account removes your personal data, your links to your children and the controls and goals you set, except records the law requires us to keep, such as payments. You can deactivate or delete your account at any time from Sign-in & security.

    Extra rules for teachers

    Last updated: 28 October 2026

    These rules are added to the Privacy policy and Terms of service for anyone using a teacher account. Keeping learners safe comes first.

    1Your profile

    • Use your real name and a photo of you. Qualifications and years of experience must be true.
    • Don't ask for or post learners' personal contact details on your profile or in resources.

    2Learners' data you can see

    You see what learners and parents choose to share with you: their name and photo, messages they send, questions and chat in your live classes, and the details a parent gives in a private lesson request. Use it only to teach them, and don't share it outside eSchool.

    3Resources you upload

    • Only upload material you made or have the right to share. You keep ownership of it.
    • By making a resource public you let eSchool show it to learners, parents and teachers on eSchool. Resources set to "Only me" can only be opened by you.
    • Resources must suit the grades you set and contain nothing unlawful, hateful or adult.

    4Live classes

    • Teach from a suitable place and dress as you would in a classroom.
    • Keep chat respectful. You can delete messages and pause chat; use them to keep the class safe.
    • Only record a class for teaching, and tell learners when you are recording. A recording is only kept if you choose to save it.

    5Private lessons and payments

    • Agree fees through eSchool. Lessons start once the parent's payment is confirmed.
    • Don't ask families to pay you outside eSchool for lessons arranged here.
    • Keep in touch with learners through eSchool, and include their parent in arrangements.
    • Book only the lessons that have been paid for, and record what happened in each one honestly.

    6Reviews

    Don't post, buy or ask for fake reviews. You may reply to a review once, politely. Reviews stay on your profile unless the person who wrote them deletes them or our team removes them. Report a review you believe breaks these terms through Help & Support.

    7Contact with learners

    • Never contact a learner in a way their parent or eSchool has not allowed.
    • We may hide content or suspend accounts that break these rules, particularly anything that puts a learner at risk.
