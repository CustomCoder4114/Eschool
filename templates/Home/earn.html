Getting started

Five steps from idea to income

From sign-up to your first paid lesson.

1

Create a Teacher Account

Register and choose the teacher role. It is free to join.

2

Fill In Your Teaching Profile

Add a headline, a short bio, your subjects and levels, your price per session and whether you teach online, in person or both.

3

Get Found

Run live classes and complete your profile. Parents see teachers matched to their child's subjects and level.

4

Accept Lesson Requests

A parent sends a request with the subject, lesson mode and preferred times. Accept it or decline it.

5

Teach and Get Paid

Once the parent's payment is confirmed, schedule the sessions and call the learner on Eschool at lesson time. The call confirms each lesson, and Eschool pays you for the lessons taught.
