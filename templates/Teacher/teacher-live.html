How a live class works

  1. 1Open the studio a few minutes early. Check your camera, microphone and the green sound bar.
  2. 2Press Go live. Learners with a reminder and learners who follow you are notified, and the class plays inside their Live Classes page.
  3. 3Share your screen for slides, read the chat, and lower hands as you answer questions.
  4. 4Press End class when you finish. Your camera and voice are recorded for Eschool (only admins can watch it). If you close the studio without ending, the class ends on its own after 15 minutes.

Video goes from your device to each learner, so a steady internet connection matters. A class whose time passes without going live moves to Past as “Not taught”; you can still teach it from there.
