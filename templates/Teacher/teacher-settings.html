Policies

The rules for teaching on Eschool, and how your data and your learners' data are handled.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. What we collect

Details you give us (your name, email address, phone number, location and your teaching profile: headline, subjects, grades, qualifications, fee and availability) and information created as you teach on Eschool, such as the resources you upload, your live classes and their recordings, your schedule, messages, reviews you receive and your replies, private lesson requests and sign-in times.

2. What learners and parents see

Your public profile shows your name, headline, subjects, grades, qualifications, public resources, live classes, rating and reviews. Your fee, lesson length and availability are shown to parents. Your email, phone number and address are never shown on your profile.
  • Resources set to "Only me" can only be opened by you.
  • Your schedule, notes and day marks are private to you.
  • Learners who join a live class see your camera, microphone or screen only while you share them. A recording is only kept if you choose to record and save it.
  • Parents you teach privately see the sessions you book and the reports you write for their child.

3. Learners' data you can see

You see what learners and parents choose to share with you: their name and photo, messages they send, questions and chat in your live classes, and the details a parent gives in a private lesson request. Use it only to teach them, and don't share it outside Eschool.

4. How we use information

  • To run Eschool, show your profile and resources, and stream your live classes
  • To send the notifications you chose, plus account and payment notices
  • To keep accounts secure (sign-in alerts, automatic sign-out)
  • To process private lesson payments (M-Pesa codes are checked by our team)

5. Sharing

We don't sell personal information. Other people see your profile only as allowed in Privacy & data. Our team may look at a resource, review or message that has been reported, to keep Eschool safe.

6. Keeping data

We keep your information while your account is open. Resources you delete are removed from Eschool. Reviews stay on your profile unless the person who wrote them deletes them or our team removes them.

7. Your choices

You can change your details, lesson details, notifications and privacy here at any time, and download a copy of your data, including your teaching profile, resources list, live classes, schedule and reviews, from Privacy & data.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to our team through Help & Support.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Agreeing to these terms

By creating a teacher account and using Eschool you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy.

2. Your account

Keep your password private. You're responsible for everything done on your account, including what you upload, say in live classes and agree with parents. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used it.

3. Your profile

  • Use your real name. Qualifications and years of experience must be true.
  • Don't ask for or post learners' personal contact details on your profile or in resources.

4. Resources you upload

  • Only upload material you made or have the right to share. You keep ownership of it.
  • By making a resource public you let Eschool show it to learners, parents and teachers on Eschool.
  • Resources must suit the grades you set and contain nothing unlawful, hateful or adult.

5. Live classes

  • Teach live classes from a suitable place and dress as you would in a classroom.
  • Keep chat respectful. You can delete messages and pause chat; use them to keep the class safe.
  • Only record a class for teaching. Tell learners when you are recording.

6. Private lessons and payments

  • Agree fees through Eschool. Sessions start once the parent's M-Pesa payment is confirmed.
  • Don't ask families to pay you outside Eschool for lessons arranged here.
  • Keep in touch with learners through Eschool, and include their parent in arrangements.
  • Book only the sessions that have been paid for, and record what happened in each one honestly.

7. Reviews

Don't post, buy or ask for fake reviews. You may reply to a review once, politely. Report a review you believe breaks these terms through Help & Support.

8. Acceptable use

  • Don't misuse Eschool, try to get around security, privacy choices or parental controls, or interfere with other people's use
  • Never contact a learner in a way their parent or Eschool has not allowed

9. Suspension

We may hide content or suspend accounts that break these terms, particularly anything that puts a learner at risk. Contact Help & Support if you'd like your account closed.

10. Service and liability

Eschool is provided as it is. We work to keep it running but can't promise it will never be interrupted. As far as the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect losses from using it.

11. Changes

We may update these terms. We'll show the new date here, and using Eschool after a change means you accept it.
