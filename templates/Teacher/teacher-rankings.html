    How ranking works

    The same rules for every teacher.

    Quality (60%)

    Reviews from parents and learners, and live class ratings. A few reviews count less than many, so one 10/10 can't put a new teacher at the top.

    Reliability (20%)

    How many booked lessons were really held. Missed lessons lower it. New teachers start at 8 until they have lessons.

    Experience (20%)

    Time teaching on eSchool (full marks at 3 years) and lessons taught (full marks at 50). Ties are broken by lessons, then reviews.
